# Constitution before feature specification

Issue: https://github.com/lambdasistemi/fido2box/issues/23

As a maintainer, I specify features against explicit architectural and recovery
rules and observe that plans and tasks enforce them before implementation.

Require small focused modules, clear dependency ownership, private extensible
records, backward compatibility, safe field interactions, and evidence-based
delivery. Synchronize the existing planning templates and contributor guidance.
Preserve ratification history and record the amendment's impact.

No expressive-record implementation, detailed feature schema, cryptographic
change, or deployment change belongs in this documentation-only amendment.
